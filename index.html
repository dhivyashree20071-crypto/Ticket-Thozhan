      How this prototype works
      1. Save route and travel date
      2. Fares are checked on a schedule (the demo clock)
      3. Today's fare is compared with stored history
      4. Open, drop and rise are detected
      5. An alert goes out by push, WhatsApp or SMS
      6. You book on the operator's own site

      Working in this prototype

      • Over 90 city pairs across India, each in both directions
      • Tracking, watchlist and reminders
      • Alert rules for open, drop, rise and last call
      • Trend forecast: a regression line over the last 7 fares, projected 3 days ahead, with a confidence score
      • Festival awareness for Diwali, Christmas, Pongal / Sankranti, Holi and Tamil New Year
      • Seat map that fills up as the travel date gets closer
      • Google Calendar reminder link

      Simulated for the demo

      • Fares and seat counts are generated from a seeded model, so every viewer sees the same numbers
      • Operator names are made up and road distances are approximate
      • Notification delivery is shown in the feed only
      • Festival dates are approximate

      Next with real data

      • Operator and aggregator APIs, or partner feeds
      • User-reported fares as a backup source
      • Firebase Cloud Messaging and WhatsApp delivery

      Help & support

      Answers on booking, payment, cancellation and travel.

      How do I book a ticket?

      Search a route and date, choose a bus, pick seats, enter passenger details, pay and receive your e-ticket with a PNR.

      How do I cancel or reschedule?

      Open My bookings, choose the trip, then Cancel or Reschedule. Reschedule is allowed once, at least 12 hours before departure.

      When do I get my refund?

      Refunds follow the slabs below and go back to the original payment method.

      What do I carry while boarding?

      Your e-ticket or PNR and a valid photo ID. Reach the boarding point 15 minutes early.

      Is payment safe?

      This is a prototype: no real payment is taken. Use the demo card shown in checkout.

      Can women or solo travellers pick seats?

      Yes. Seats booked by women show in pink and seats booked by men show in blue. If a woman books alone, the seat next to her is women-only. If a man books alone, the seat next to him is men-only. Those seats show a dashed border and are blocked for the other gender.
